# Polish art prompts

These are new pictures. Do not regenerate the art-v6 stunt set (`deck_red`, `loop_track`, `plywood_lanes`, `diamond`, `truss_yellow`, `truss_blue`, `chevron_yellow_black`, `hazard_red_white`) or the field set (`grass_a`, `grass_b`, `grass_flowers`, `dirt`, `dirt_rutted`, `sand`, `asphalt`, `curb`, `ramp_planks`, `concrete`, and the tuft, rock, and hedge sprites). The sky stays a coded gradient. No sky plate, no cloud atlas.

Each prompt is one image. Square unless the size says otherwise. No text, no watermark, no border, no people.

## Gap pond

The gap course has a flat blue disc in the sand. It needs a surface, not a new sky.

Prompt: Top-down seamless water texture, 1024 square. Pale swimming-pool blue with soft caustic ripples, a few lighter streaks, and a slightly darker rim. Toy and clean, not a photograph of the ocean. No horizon, no objects, no foam, no fish. The tile must repeat on all four edges.

## Jeep windshield

The red jeep's windshield is a blank white pane. The body paint is already on the model.

Prompt: Square 1024 texture for a toy jeep windshield. Mostly clear with a faint pale-blue sky reflection across the upper third and a soft gray interior behind the glass: two dark seats and a simple dash. Edges fade to transparent. No text, no driver, no frame, no background scene.

## Monster-truck wheel

The monster truck and the jeep share smooth, low-poly tires. One wheel picture can be mapped on the existing wheel.

Prompt: Square 1024, orthographic side view of one chunky toy monster-truck wheel, centered and filling the frame. Deep black rubber tread with big blocky knobs, and a glossy red steel rim with simple round holes. Even lighting, no ground shadow, no axle, no text. Flat neutral gray background.

## Jeep wheel

Prompt: Square 1024, orthographic side view of one smaller toy jeep wheel, centered. Black rubber with a modest all-terrain tread and a glossy red rim, slightly smaller and less knobby than a monster truck. Even lighting, no ground shadow, no text. Flat neutral gray background.

## Boost pad

The boost arrows are flat yellow cones sitting on the asphalt.

Prompt: Top-down decal, 512 wide by 1024 tall. Dark asphalt with three bold yellow chevron arrows pointing to the top of the image, painted like a toy stencil, slightly worn. No words. The short top and bottom edges should be able to sit on asphalt without a hard cut.

## Launch lip

The red ramp ends in untextured black and white boxes.

Prompt: Top-down strip, 1024 wide by 128 tall. A painted black-and-white checker band for the lip of a toy ramp, slightly scuffed, matte, not a photo of cloth. No text. Repeat cleanly along the long edge.
